License Information · Family-Owned & Operated · Fully Licensed and Insured Moving Company · Moving Customers Coast-to-Coast Since 1997

VIP Packing and Moving Services is a fully licensed and insured moving company headquartered in Columbia, South Carolina. Full moves are available nationwide; labor-only services (packing, loading, unloading) are available in South Carolina, North Carolina, and Georgia. Every job is supervised on-site to ensure customers receive the best service possible. All estimates are non-binding and based on the information provided at time of quote. Final pricing reflects actual time, distance, packing materials, additional services, and any access challenges encountered during the move. Long-distance and out-of-state moves are handled directly by our own crew — your belongings are never subcontracted, transferred between trucks, or driven by third parties. For questions about your rights and responsibilities when moving, please contact us at (803) 929-0656.
